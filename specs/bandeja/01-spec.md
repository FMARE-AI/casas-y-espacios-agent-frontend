# 01 - Bandeja de Entrada Spec

## WHAT
Implement the "Bandeja de Entrada" (Inbox) page for the Casas y Espacios internal web panel. The page will display active and escalated WhatsApp conversations as cards, providing real-time metrics, filtering capabilities, and the ability for advisors to "take" (assign to themselves) escalated conversations.

## WHY
To allow operational advisors to monitor bot activity, identify escalated conversations, and manually intervene when necessary. Administrative users also need to see aggregate metrics across the inbox to monitor capacity and bottlenecks.
